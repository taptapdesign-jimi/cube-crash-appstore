Game Support

Official help and contact information for Stack to Six, independently designed and developed by Igor Jimi Ivankovic, the creator behind Tap Tap Design.

Contact support

For gameplay, technical, accessibility, or privacy help, email:

stacktosix@gmail.com

When reporting a problem, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, game mode (Journey or Arcade), the stage or round, steps that reproduce the problem, and a screenshot if available. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Quick answers

  • No account is required: Stack to Six can be played without registration or sign-in.
  • Progress is local: progress, scores, saved games, and preferences are stored on your device.
  • 3D Motion is optional: turn the visual tilt effect on or off under Settings → 3D Motion.
  • No purchases or ads: the current version has no advertising or in-app purchases.
  • Offline play: the current game is bundled with the app and does not require a gameplay server.
