Privacy Policy

Stack to Six was independently designed and developed by Igor Jimi Ivankovic, the creator behind Tap Tap Design. The game is built to be played without creating an account and without giving us personal data.

Effective August 27, 2026

1. Privacy summary

The current version of Stack to Six does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data. The game does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party advertising and analytics SDKs.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the game.

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or identifiers are collected.
  • No advertising identifier is accessed or used.
  • No gameplay or diagnostic analytics are sent to Tap Tap Design.
  • No information is used to track you across apps or websites.

3. Data stored on your device

Game progress, scores, unlocked content, saved games, and preferences are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide game features and is not transmitted to Tap Tap Design.

Local game data remains on the device until it is reset by the game or the app is deleted. Device backup and restore behavior is controlled by your operating system and backup settings.

4. Third-party services and Game Center

The current version does not integrate Apple Game Center, advertising networks, analytics services, crash-reporting services, social login, or in-app purchases. If a future version introduces a service that changes how data is handled, this policy and the App Store privacy declarations will be updated before that version is released.

5. Children's privacy

Stack to Six does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other players. Because the game has no account system and does not transmit personal data to Tap Tap Design, players are not asked to provide personal details.

6. Data sharing and retention

Tap Tap Design has no game server receiving player information from Stack to Six. We therefore do not retain or share player data on our servers. Data stored locally is controlled by the player through the game, device settings, and app deletion.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game's features or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.
